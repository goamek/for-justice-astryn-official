Mechanics Breakdown · 04

Writing & Narrative Branching

How It Works

The story ends with a choice. After Vorkoth falls, Astryn decides whether to spare him or kill him, and that choice, along with the bond she built with Novius earlier in the game, leads to one of three endings. Before the duel can start, the player has to talk to all three of Astryn's teammates. Once an ending with a post-credits scene has been seen, the main menu unlocks a button to replay that scene for the rest of the session.

Branch 1: Kill Vorkoth

When Vorkoth falls, he is given a real way out. Astryn tells him to step down and return what he has stolen from the city, and he refuses, insisting that his extortion and tariffs were simply how a city gets run. That refusal is what the kill ending rests on. Astryn then draws a pistol from a holster on her thigh, a weapon she never uses in combat, and the team says nothing. Nobody tells her to do it, and nobody stops her.

Astryn whispers "For justice," a single shot rings out, and Vorkoth vanishes from the arena before the screen fades to the credits. A post-credits scene follows, where Aegrandir reminds her that not every fight will end like this one. That scene exists on purpose: the kill is presented as the right call for this one man, not as the game's answer to every problem.

Branch 2: Spare Vorkoth (Novius Intervenes)

The player only ever sees two options, spare or kill. If they choose to spare Vorkoth, the game checks whether Astryn and Novius bonded earlier, which happens in a second, optional conversation with Novius during free roam. If they did, this ending plays.

Astryn lowers the pistol, tells Vorkoth he is not worth it, and turns her back. Vorkoth sneers "...Mercy. From you.", but Novius steps in and cuts him down. Astryn thanks him, and Novius tells her not to spare men like Vorkoth again. Vorkoth dies either way, so mercy is never rewarded by a change of heart on his part. The only thing that saves Astryn is the trust she built with her teammate, which makes the optional conversation matter to the outcome. A separate post-credits scene plays here, where Aegrandir tells her that her choice still counts even though it did not go the way she wanted.

Watch this ending

Branch 3: Spare Vorkoth (Astryn Falls)

If Astryn spares Vorkoth without having bonded with Novius, nobody is there to stop him. She holsters the pistol and walks away, and Vorkoth, sneering "...Mercy. From you.", teleports behind her and strikes her down. The screen cuts to black, and Vorkoth's voice is heard shouting to the crowd that Levaris does not bow to radicals and that this "Justice" nonsense will die with her.

Astryn gets no last line here, unlike the kill ending. Mercy costs her even that. This is the game's failure state, so there is no post-credits scene, and the player is returned to free roam with their progress intact. It is intentionally understated instead of punishing, and it makes the point that Vorkoth is not a man who can be shown mercy.
