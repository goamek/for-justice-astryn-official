Overview

A fractured nation, and one rebellion within it

For Justice: Astryn is a 2.5D, turn-based JRPG set in a semi-modern fantasy world where the balance of magic is unraveling under systemic corruption. After the targeted assassination of her closest ally, Thyone, Astryn begins a journey of personal and political rebellion aimed at dismantling an oppressive, militaristic fascist regime. Free-roam exploration surfaces the stark socio-economic inequality and cultural struggle faced by all peoples who share this fractured nation, while mana, the source of every spell cast, draws its strength from the bonds formed between allies.
